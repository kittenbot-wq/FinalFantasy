Audio is 40% of the learning experience.

The speaker selection is the heart of any sound system design, be it a concert hall, a church, or a classroom. Soundbars can be a wonderful solution to installation and space issues, but that typical soundbar from your local audio or discount store was designed for a living room or residential entertainment system. With maybe 6 kids on a busy Saturday, you’re probably OK with that in your 200 sq. ft. home TV room, but a US classroom is typically 5 times larger than the average residential living room.

"GOOD ENOUGH" IS NOT PART OF OUR MANTRA

Leading Edge is the first company to address this problem, and our soundbar eliminates the need for complicated and costly installation. Simple one-cable connection.

If you are dedicated to a student's successful education, just "good enough" is NOT good enough. Our signature sound is simply amazing. It carries through every one of our products. Vocals are stunningly crisp with just the right amount of bass that makes voices sound comfortable all day and doesn’t require a subwoofer that would rattle the classroom next door. We don’t build our speaker systems to favor car chases or booms and bangs. And this is the first soundbar to be specially designed for maximum loudness before feedback with wireless teacher-worn microphones.

KARTBar™

Mobile sound should be noble sound

Your beautiful new interactive display panel possibly has little 1” x 2” speakers ( usually at the bottom of the board) which are just not going to project into a 1000 sq ft. classroom. Even 40 watts into tiny speakers don’t make them produce room-filling audio. It's not just a question of loudness, but clarity with minimal distortion.

The sound source, in this case the soundbar, needs to be above the video display but angled down to the students throughout the classroom.  The KARTBar is perfect for mobile carts, which all need quality sound. Just add a compact power amp and you have an extraordinary sound that goes anywhere.

 

High Definition Audio is on a roll

Interactive display panels have revolutionized the classroom, but they often have not kept pace with the audio potential.  Mobile carts present a whole new challenge to audio, but the KARTBar was designed especially for this task with tilted mounting bars and a lightweight package that will interface so easily with virtually every display panel's back panel VESA mounts.

Stereo WUNDERBar™

We’ve looked at this from all angles

 

Our Stereo WUNDERBar has an angled front panel that spreads the sound across the entire width of a classroom. The Stereo WUNDERBar is the perfect answer for an easy to mount, amazing-sounding true stereo speaker system.

Its trim 5” height fits nicely above interactive Display Panels or video projector installations.

The Stereo WUNDERBar offers the ultimate in full range voice performance, yet remains excellent for reproducing musical content. Subwoofers should not be a requirement for great soundbars.

No molded thin wall plastic boxes here. Real high-performance transducers and precise, complex crossover networks are combined with meticulous hand-built cabinetry. We utilize professional-level components that offer the best performance for a classroom environment.

At only 19 lbs, the Stereo WUNDERBar installation is safe and easy with any dual channel power amplifier system.

MINIBar™

Give all your students a Leading Edge in listening and learning

Our MINIBar is perfect for slightly smaller rooms where stereo is not required but clarity and full sound is certainly desired.   The MINIBar continues our signature sound but is designed and manufactured in a compact, very affordable package weighing only 11 lbs.

The MINIBar features  VESA compliant attachment points for wall mounting and with our optional interactive panel mounting bars, it also can be mounted on a large TV/ display panel with  600 mm x 400mm VESA fly points.

About Us

Leading Edge Sound Arrays is a collaboration of 3 audio professionals with over 100 years of combined experience in the design, sales, and manufacturing of professional audio.   Our systems are designed and assembled in the USA with worldwide components.  In a video-rich world, we are totally committed to providing stunning vocal audio for the classroom environment.  We have spent years listening to sound installers, school technicians, audiologists, teachers, and administrators and we want to make sure every word is always heard.

Frank Grund is the co-owner of Grundorf Corporation which is the manufacturing facility where all Leading Edge Sound Arrays are assembled.  Grundorf has been building high-quality speaker enclosures for over 30 years in their Council Bluffs, Iowa factory.   Frank lends vast technical experience and testing facilities for the final perfection of our systems.  Frank also has a background as a Midwest touring musician and also an educator in recording processes and techniques.  He also currently maintains a 24 track home studio and occasionally records aspiring local area music acts.

Jerry Hogerson is the concept designer of Leading Edge Sound Arrays.   Jerry’s concert audio mixing and musician background led to working with Altec Lansing in the 1970s developing professional audio products and later, sales management positions.  In 1977, Jerry started his sales rep firm, Star Enterprises, and for 30 years, represented such companies as  JBL, Soundcraft,  and Audio Technica.  In 2007, Jerry began a lengthy relationship with TeachLogic, a manufacturer of wireless microphone-based sound systems for the classroom.   All this combined into a lifelong experience of working with loudspeakers and their interactions with microphones. Star Enterprises is the exclusive manufacturer’s sales representative for Leading Edge Sound Arrays in Southern California, Arizona, Hawaii, and Southern Nevada.

John Grandinetti is the National Sales Director of Leading Edge Sound Arrays.  A native Nebraskan like Jerry, John followed his music dreams to California in the 1970s.   His decades-long sales background around the world was varied in all aspects of both retail and factory sales of loudspeakers, microphones and music equipment working with such companies as Cerwin Vega, InMusic Brands and Samson Technologies. He brings a wealth of sales relationship experience to the team.  We are a people company and John will be reaching out to many of audio and education’s best.   John is also a terrific writer of fiction and non-fiction and his books are available on Amazon under his name.
