Field notes on a very strange fish

  • Dad

    The male carries the eggs in a brood pouch and gives birth, sometimes to more than a thousand young at once.

  • 35 Hz

    The dorsal fin on the back flutters dozens of times a second. It is the only engine they have.

  • 1.5 m/h

    Top speed of the dwarf seahorse, H. zosterae, often cited as the slowest fish in the sea.

  • 0

    Stomachs. No teeth either. Food passes straight through, so they eat almost constantly.

  • Square

    The tail is square in cross-section, built from bony plates, and grips seagrass like a hand.

  • Daily

    Bonded pairs greet each other every morning with a slow dance, sometimes changing color as they go.
